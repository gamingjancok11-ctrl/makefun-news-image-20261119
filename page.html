1 · Diplomacy · Shenzhen

Trump, Xi and Putin hold first-ever three-way summit

SHENZHEN — The leaders of the United States, China and Russia meet face to face for the first time as a trio on the APEC sidelines. Nuclear arms control, Ukraine and Iran top the agenda; a joint statement promises UN and ICC reform, but no major agreement is announced. Ukraine and its European allies are not at the table.

2 · Justice · The Hague

World court rules Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya

THE HAGUE — The International Court of Justice rules 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 military campaign — the first time the court has found a state guilty of genocide. Amnesty International calls the verdict “historic”. Rohingya refugees watch the ruling from Cox’s Bazar in Bangladesh.

3 · War · Crimea

Ukraine strikes Kerch Bridge a third time; Putin rules out ceasefire

KERCH — Ukrainian Neptune missiles hit the railway section of the Crimean Bridge; an arch and road span collapse into the water and train traffic halts. Putin, in Shenzhen, rules out ceasefire talks and vows retaliation. Occupation authorities announce free ferries between Kerch and Taman from Friday.
